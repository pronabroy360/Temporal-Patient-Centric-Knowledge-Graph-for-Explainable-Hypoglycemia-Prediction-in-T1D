# DCLP3/DCLP5 temporal insulin–CGM coverage screen

**Audit date:** 31 October 2026  
**Scope:** local public-use DCLP3 release 3 and DCLP5 tables  
**Scope/status:** This document records structural and window-eligibility
screens only. A separate frozen Loop CGM-only transfer diagnostic was later
completed and is documented in
[frozen_loop_cgm_jaeb_transfer_20261031.md](frozen_loop_cgm_jaeb_transfer_20261031.md).
No insulin/event or graph model predictions are reported for DCLP3/DCLP5.

## Decision

DCLP3 and DCLP5 are plausible candidates for a **separate insulin–CGM
external evaluation**: after sorting and applying a strict two-hour-history,
30-minute-future window rule, both provide millions of eligible windows with
basal and bolus records in the lookback for many windows. Neither release has
usable Roche carbohydrate records in the screened tables, so neither can
replicate the meal-aware Loop experiment as currently specified. The later
CGM-only transfer diagnostic does not validate insulin/event or graph models.

## Strict window-eligibility screen

The follow-up machine-readable aggregate is
[`audit/dclp_eligible_windows_20261031.json`](../audit/dclp_eligible_windows_20261031.json),
generated by
[`scripts/audit_jaeb_eligible_windows.py`](../scripts/audit_jaeb_eligible_windows.py).
It requires 25 numeric, normal-status CGM readings spanning 120 minutes through
the prediction anchor, six fully observed future readings spanning 30 minutes,
300-second nominal cadence with every adjacent gap within ±60 seconds, and no
conflicting same-time records. The outcome is any future numeric glucose below
70 mg/dL. Timestamped basal and bolus events are counted in the preceding 120
minutes using occurrence time; their actual availability to a predictor is
unknown.

The initial pass used raw `DataDtTm` for CGM (DCLP3's selected CGM table has no
adjusted field; the DCLP5 table does) and preferred `DataDtTm_adjusted` for
pump events when populated. This gives DCLP5 a mixed clock basis. The counts
below are therefore an initial timestamp-policy-specific screen, not a
finalized cross-release eligibility result; DCLP5 alternatives are quantified
in the policy table below.

| Release | CGM participants screened | Eligible windows | Positive windows | Positive prevalence | Windows with basal + bolus in 120-minute lookback | Windows with neither |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| DCLP3 post-randomization | 168 | 7,930,123 | 241,856 | 3.05% | 2,782,216 | 2,399,306 |
| DCLP5, numeric-only primary | 100 | 5,113,177 | 168,370 | 3.29% | 2,702,136 | 138,966 |

DCLP3's 602,103 baseline records were excluded from the primary screen,
including the release-documented participant with approximately two weeks of
pre-admission CGM whose de-identified dates are not aligned to enrollment.
The DCLP5 canonicalization collapsed 1,628 exact same-time duplicate rows and
removed 26 same-time groups with conflicting values or status; 60 source rows
had unparseable CGM timestamps. Its 3,275 explicit low-indicator sentinel rows
were excluded from numeric inputs and primary numeric labels. A separate
sensitivity that permits explicit low sentinels in the six future samples
produced 5,118,085 eligible windows and 173,297 positives (3.39%); this is a
censored-low indication, not a numeric reading.

## Pump-value and source-timestamp semantics screen

The Jaeb DCLP5 data glossary describes `Pump_BasalRateChange` as one record
per basal-rate change and `CommandedBasalRate` as the new basal rate. It
describes `Pump_BolusDelivered` as one record per pump-delivered bolus,
`InsulinDelivered` as the amount actually delivered, and `BolusType` as
Standard or Extended. The release's extracted bolus file instead names its
amount field `BolusAmount`; resolve this schema-name difference against the
release documentation or Jaeb before treating it as a confirmed cross-release
mapping. The DCLP3 files also use `CommandedBasalRate` and `BolusAmount`.
DCLP3's readme and the DCLP5 glossary describe `DataDtTm_adjusted` as local
time including Jaeb adjustments. These definitions support treating basal
records as state-change candidates, but do not specify state end times,
timezone equivalence between releases, or when adjusted values became
available to a predictor.

The follow-up aggregate
[`audit/dclp_insulin_semantics_20261031.json`](../audit/dclp_insulin_semantics_20261031.json),
generated by [`scripts/audit_dclp_insulin_semantics.py`](../scripts/audit_dclp_insulin_semantics.py),
checks numeric validity and source timestamp-field differences. All screened
basal-rate and bolus-amount rows are numeric and nonnegative. DCLP3 has
2,610,772 basal-rate rows (median 0.966, 103,962 zero values) and 221,292
bolus rows (4,257 marked Extended, 217,035 Standard); DCLP5 has 2,531,963
basal-rate rows (median 0.760, 159,686 zero values) and 226,685 bolus rows
(3,169 Extended, 223,516 Standard). These are source-value summaries. The
glossary establishes that DCLP5's bolus field represents insulin actually
delivered, but the current adapter must confirm which file field corresponds
to that glossary field. The audit itself does not infer units or interpret
zero as suspension.

The adjusted timestamp differs from its paired raw timestamp in 38,382 DCLP3
basal rows, 3,107 DCLP3 bolus rows, 65,190 DCLP5 basal rows, and 6,122 DCLP5
bolus rows. Naive subtraction of the parsed strings shows DCLP3 shifts are
dominated by 12–24 hours (25,833 basal and 2,166 bolus rows); 1,571 basal and
156 bolus rows differ by over 24 hours, with a maximum difference of
337,341,360 seconds. DCLP5 has a more mixed distribution: 29,749 basal and
3,144 bolus shifts are 5 minutes to 1 hour, while 225 basal and 100 bolus
shifts exceed 24 hours. The largest DCLP5 differences are also extreme
(86,400 seconds for basal; 31,535,941 for bolus). These are raw-versus-adjusted
string deltas only: the fields may not share a clock basis, and the audit
cannot tell whether an extreme is correction, de-identification, or a source
problem. Do not automatically prefer one field as a common cross-release
clock without resolving these differences. DCLP3 and DCLP5 Extended bolus
labels also need a prespecified compatible mapping. The source definitions
were checked against local DCLP3 `ReadMe.rtf` and DCLP5 `DataGlossary_Dits.rtf`;
those files are not included in the aggregate review package.

These counts support a release-specific **insulin-only transfer evaluation**
feasibility route, conditional on the current timestamp policy. They do not
establish correctly aligned cross-release windows, event equivalence, observed
event availability, treatment-arm comparability, or model performance. The
timestamp-derived eligibility and overlap counts must be recomputed once an
authoritatively justified clock policy is chosen. A Loop-trained insulin-only
model/input contract must then be frozen before evaluating either release.

## Interpretation limits

“Inside CGM span” means only that an event's timestamp falls between that
participant's earliest and latest parsed CGM timestamps. It does not prove
CGM was continuously worn at the event time, that the event was recorded
completely, or that it was available to a predictor at that time. The audit
assumes the release's local/de-identified timestamp ordering and assigns no
timezone. It does not establish treatment-period comparability, define a
cross-study endpoint or validate a model.

DCLP3's readme specifically warns that one participant's pre-admission CGM
dates are shifted and not accurately aligned to enrollment. A release adapter
must isolate or exclude that period before event alignment. For both studies,
an external validation protocol must freeze the cohort, timestamp policy,
CGM sentinel handling, eligibility rules, and basal/bolus event mappings
before accessing model outcomes.

## Remaining external-validation gate

1. Resolve each dataset's source-specific clock correction, units, and event
   semantics from its authoritative data dictionary/protocol. In particular,
   explain the large mixed raw-to-adjusted timestamp deltas, determine whether
   basal-rate changes define state intervals, how zero rates and Extended
   boluses should be represented, and whether a rate at time *t* is available
   for the following interval without future information.
2. Freeze a Loop-trained insulin-only CGM baseline and event model, including
   feature scaling, CGM sentinel policy, basal state reconstruction, bolus
   encoding, missing-event handling, and the exact 120-minute/30-minute
   contract. Do not fit on DCLP outcome labels.
3. Rebuild the external windows with a separate adapter and verify emitted
   input/label identity and temporal order before scoring; do not select a
   dataset or policy based on external performance.
4. Report DCLP3/DCLP5 as external insulin-only evaluations only after source
   semantics and parity gates pass. Such results would not validate meal
   relations, graph novelty, or clinical utility.

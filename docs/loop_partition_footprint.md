# Loop minimal-column partition footprint

Status: completed 13 September 2026. The read-only, identifier-free evidence is [loop_partition_footprint.json](../audit/loop_partition_footprint.json), generated by `scripts/estimate_loop_partition_footprint.py`.

## Result

The full scan projected a 64-way, pipe-delimited partition containing only UTC occurrence time, source and upload IDs, and canonical-key fields. It did not write any partitions or transformed clinical records.

| **Total** | **163,599,901** | **10,413,318,793** | — |

The projected data total is 9.70 GiB. At measurement time the workspace had 48 GiB free. A conservative two-copy allowance for projected partitions and sort scratch is 19.40 GiB, leaving about 28.6 GiB. The release itself is already included in used disk space and is never copied.

## Decision

Proceed locally with a **one-modality-at-a-time, 64-way hashed-partition audit**. Each stage must delete only its own disposable partition files after producing an aggregate report, before beginning the next modality. The audit must retain no patient identifiers in final reports and must stop if free space falls below the predeclared safety floor.

This decision permits the final participant-sorted duplicate and coverage audit. It does not authorize window construction, model training, use of upload time as observed availability, or a full raw-data export.

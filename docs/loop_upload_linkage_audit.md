# Loop upload-linkage audit

Audit date: 10 September 2026. This audit tested whether `ParentLOOPDeviceUploadsID` and `UploadUTCDtTm` can provide first-usable event times for the Loop cohort.

## Decision

The release **does not pass the observed availability-time gate** for the primary cohort. Keep occurrence time as the core as-of-time basis under the explicitly stated immediate-availability assumption. Evaluate delayed auxiliary-event availability only as a prespecified simulation. Do not describe Loop upload time as measured real-world data-arrival time.

## Linkage findings

`LOOPDeviceUploads.txt` has 99,102 unique upload IDs, no duplicate IDs, and 9,550 records without an upload UTC timestamp. All nonempty upload timestamps follow the expected UTC string format.

| Modality | Event rows | Linked upload ID | Valid upload UTC not before event | Reason it fails the observed-latency gate |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| CGM | 111,118,148 | 109,184,728 | 6,834,706 | 78,674,248 linked rows lack a usable upload UTC; 23,675,774 have upload time earlier than event time |
| Basal | 48,301,308 | 48,143,402 | 325,728 | 47,817,332 linked rows lack usable upload UTC |
| Bolus | 2,722,513 | 2,703,803 | 132,710 | 2,560,429 linked rows lack usable upload UTC |
| Food | 1,406,204 | 1,403,485 | 305 | 1,402,691 linked rows lack usable upload UTC |
| Exercise | 51,728 | 50,608 | 103 | 50,379 linked rows lack usable upload UTC |
| Wizard | 119,354 | 111,567 | 108,755 | This isolated field is comparatively complete, but it does not rescue the shared multimodal availability experiment |

“Valid upload UTC not before event” means the parent link resolves, the upload timestamp is present and well formed, and its UTC value is greater than or equal to the event UTC value. It does not prove that the record was first usable then. The result is inadequate for a representative, information-matched delay analysis because filtering to this subset would discard most CGM, basal, bolus, food and exercise data and change the cohort.

The sampled CGM upload-minus-event lags have a negative median, further showing that the two timestamp fields cannot be interpreted naively as clinical occurrence followed by system arrival. This may reflect de-identification, source clocks, incomplete upload history, or other export behavior; this audit does not identify a single cause.

## Revised information-availability design

1. Main benchmark: use event occurrence times with `assumed_immediate` availability, consistently for all models.
2. Delay sensitivity analysis: hide auxiliary event records until prespecified simulated delays; retain occurrence times and give every comparator the identical admissible event set.
3. Do not simulate a per-event missing flag or alter CGM availability through this auxiliary-event scenario.
4. Report the scenario as sensitivity to assumed reporting delay, not measured latency or deployment performance.
5. Retain upload identifiers and upload timestamps as provenance only. They may support a later source-specific investigation after additional documentation, but not the primary claim.

The identifier-free report is [audit/loop_upload_linkage.json](../audit/loop_upload_linkage.json), generated by `scripts/audit_loop_upload_linkage.py`.

# Supplementary numerical results

Revision: 8 November 2026. Saved evidence through 31 October 2026. This appendix includes all 21 central paired comparisons. It is not a count of every historical fit. The full-precision [CSV](results_snapshot.csv) and [evidence map](evidence_map.json) preserve source links. Differences are graph minus the paired comparator; intervals condition on fixed fitted predictions. AP uses positive-outcome participants and macro-Brier uses all participants. No numerical result is regenerated by training in this documentation build.

## Two-seed participant summary

Each participant's paired effects were averaged across the two fixed seeds before participant resampling. This differs from averaging fold APs and does not make the seeds independent cohorts. The mean AP difference was -0.0098996662, with interval [-0.01047669424739838, -0.009345412841435134]; macro-Brier difference was 0.0001264322, with interval [0.0001096006568634377, 0.0001428661822493643].

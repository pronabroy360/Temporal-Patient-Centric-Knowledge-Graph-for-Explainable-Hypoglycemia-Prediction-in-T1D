\section*{6. Selected working journal format}
JAMIA \textbf{Research and Applications} is the working format selected on 8 November 2026. The \href{https://academic.oup.com/jamia/pages/General_Instructions}{official instructions} specify main text up to 4,000 words, a structured abstract up to 250 words, up to 4 tables and 6 figures, and no more than 5 keywords. The present main paper uses three tables, two figures with alt text, five keywords and the prescribed abstract headings. Body and abstract counts are reported by the dossier verifier; a conservative body count includes declarations and legends while excluding table rows. The Word and LaTeX versions are double-spaced, and figure legends are at the end. This is format preparation, not a prediction of editorial acceptance.

The official instructions' citation eligibility language requires editorial confirmation for the relevant thesis, conference proposal and preprints. These works materially constrain novelty and cannot be silently omitted or presented as peer-reviewed journal evidence. Their status remains explicit in the references and source review. AI disclosure must also appear in the eventual cover letter. A data-availability statement and author-approved CRediT roles are required. The internal TRIPOD+AI crosswalk is adopted as reporting best practice; no unverified blanket journal mandate is claimed.
